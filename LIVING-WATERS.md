# Trial #01 · Living Waters Phase: a first outline

*Status: rough ideas only. Planned properly once the Book Phase reaches its resting place.*

## The aim
The book feels completely integrated with whatever AI model it's connected to. **Online, it's an AI app.
Offline, it's still a practically just-as-powerful app.** The book is the vessel; the AI is the breath in it.

## What "integrated" could mean (to decide together)
- **One voice, no separate place.** You don't go to a chat window; you talk to the book where you're writing,
  and it answers and acts right there, in the page and in the folds.
- **It acts, not just talks.** It can write into folds, fold your words, make questions, run a drill, change the
  look, change the app itself, with Undo for everything it does.
- **It knows the book.** It can see the page you're on, the folds around it, and where things came from (the
  family tree of folds), so it answers in context.
- **Any model.** Claude by default; other models (your other AI subscriptions, a model on your own computer) can
  be plugged in the same way.
- **Your words stay yours.** It only sends what it needs, only when asked, and never adds writing time.

## Offline: just as powerful
- Everything you write, fold and practise works with no internet.
- What needs the AI waits politely: requests you make offline are queued and done when you're back online
  ("Ask Claude when I'm online").
- Possible later: a small model that runs on the device itself for simple help offline.

## What already exists (from the earlier build)
- Claude in the book (✦ Claude, Ctrl/⌘ K): streaming replies, tools over the book's own actions, Undo.
- AI feedback on memory tests, AI-written questions, "Write deeper with AI" folds.
- Your own Anthropic key, a monthly spending limit, a Workspace ID box, Haiku / Sonnet / Opus.
- Add-ons and app versions Claude can make from inside the book.
These were built before the Book Phase; Living Waters will rebuild them around the new page, one piece at a
time, the same way.

## Questions for later
1. What should the AI do on its own, and what only when you ask?
2. Where does it "appear" on the one living page: in a fold, in the bar, beside the words?
3. How should it feel when you're offline: quiet, or reminding you what's waiting?
4. Which models do you want connected first?
